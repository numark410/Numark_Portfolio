04 / HOW I WORK

From symptom
to solution.

Good troubleshooting is not guessing. It is a process of observation, measurement, isolation, verification and improvement.

01

Observe

Understand the symptom, behavior and operating conditions before touching the system.

02

Measure

Use measurements and evidence to identify what is actually happening.

03

Isolate

Narrow the fault from system level to subsystem, circuit and component.

04

Repair

Correct the underlying problem rather than simply treating the symptom.

05

Verify

Test the result under realistic conditions and confirm stable operation.

06

Improve

Where appropriate, identify ways to make the system safer, stronger or easier to maintain.
